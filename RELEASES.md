# Releases

Newest first. Under each `## vX.Y.Z` heading, write one short message for users, in plain words.
When a newer release is out, Claude shows these messages to the user word for word and asks
whether to update. A user who skipped some releases sees the messages of all of them.

## v0.5.3

Labels and printed letters now draw the same way every time, so a video no longer fails its last check
because a label's edge changed by a pixel. There is a new helper, `g.print()`, that puts a letter or a
number on a clay model.

## v0.5.2

The gallery page now works on phones. On a phone you scroll, and the page plays: every scene fits the
screen, and in the scene with his 12 moves he walks to the strawberry, kneels and points. Nothing changes on a
computer or in how you make a video.

## v0.5.1

Clayducation now calls itself an AI video engine built on Claude Code, in the setup, the README and the
gallery page. Nothing changes in how you make a video.

## v0.5.0

The mascot has six new actions for your videos: he kicks a ball, jumps, hammers a nail (and pulls it out),
sweeps with a broom, waters a plant that grows and flowers, and lies down to rest. The gallery page has a new
scene where you can click anything and he walks over and does it.

## v0.4.1

When a new release is out, Claude now tells you what changed and asks whether to update.

## v0.4.0

Clayducation now installs releases, and checks once a day for a newer one.
